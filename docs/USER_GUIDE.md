# Using Flick

Flick is a scratchpad for text you want to keep on your iPhone.

## Save a note

1. Type or paste text into the note field.
2. Tap **Save Note**. At larger accessibility text sizes, the button says **Save**.
3. **Saved** confirms that your text has been saved on your device.

Your notes appear below the editor, newest first. Tap a note to read it and
see the original text. Swipe left on a note to delete it, then confirm.

## If something needs another try

- **Couldn't save note. Your text is still here.** Keep Flick open to retain the
  unsaved draft, then tap **Try again**.
- **Saved · Finishing note…** Your text is saved while Flick finishes the note.
- **Saved · Note not ready.** Your text is saved. Tap **Retry** on that row.
- **Couldn't open notes.** Tap **Try again** to reopen them.

Flick follows your device's light or dark appearance and text-size settings.
At larger text sizes, scroll to move between the editor, Save button, and notes.
